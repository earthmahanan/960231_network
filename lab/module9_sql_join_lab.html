Two tables walk into a query. What comes out depends entirely on you.

Student info

🍿
Why this lab feels like a dating show. A JOIN is just SQL trying to set two tables up on a date based on a shared value (the key). INNER JOIN only invites the couples who actually matched. LEFT JOIN makes sure every row from the left table shows up — matched or not, third-wheeling with a table full of NULLs. FULL OUTER JOIN is the "everybody's invited, no one left behind" version. Keep that picture in your head — you'll use it in every exercise below.
